Iguazú Family Survival & Packing Guide

Crucial advice for Charles (age 6), Felix (age 4), and grandparents in the subtropical jungle

🦝

Rule #1: Beware the Cute Coatis!

The South American coatis (coatíes) wandering the park look adorable, but they are bold wild scavengers with sharp claws. Never let kids hold snacks or food in their hands outside. Keep all food zipped tight inside backpacks. Eat only inside enclosed cafe patios!

🌧️

Waterproof Ponchos & Phone Bags

At the Devil's Throat balcony and Upper Circuit lookouts, the waterfall spray can completely soak you depending on wind direction. Bring lightweight disposable rain ponchos for all 6 family members and waterproof ziplock bags/lanyards for phones!

🦟

DEET Bug Spray & Strollers

Iguazú is a warm subtropical jungle. Pack mosquito repellent (green Off! with DEET) and apply before hitting the trails. Good news: both the Devil's Throat walkway and the Upper Circuit are 100% metal-grated flat boardwalks that easily accommodate strollers!
