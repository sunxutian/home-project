How to Pay in Buenos Aires (MEP Rate Explained)

Foreign Visa & Mastercard credit cards automatically receive the favorable MEP tourist exchange rate. You do not need to carry stacks of cash or exchange on Florida Street. Use a no-foreign-transaction-fee US credit card for almost all dining, Uber, supermarkets, and shopping. Carry ~$100–$150 USD in crisp $20s for cash tips (propina ~10%).

Family Survival Guide & Quick Spanish Helper

Crucial local knowledge for stress-free dining and getting around with kids

1. Argentine Dining Hours

Locals dine late (9:30 PM to 11:30 PM). Parrillas and restaurants typically open for dinner between 7:30 PM and 8:00 PM. As a traveling family with kids, arriving at 7:45 PM is your superpower: no waitlists, quiet dining rooms, and rapid food service!

2. What Kids Love to Eat

Every Argentine restaurant has kid favorites: Milanesa con papas fritas (tender breaded beef or chicken cutlet with french fries), Empanadas de jamón y queso (ham & cheese), Ñoquis (potato gnocchi), and Helado de Dulce de Leche.
